How booking works

  1. Search a route. Enter your pickup and drop-off locations to see carriers already driving that way with space to spare.
  2. Pick an available route. Compare space left, schedule, and starting price, then choose the one that fits your shipment.
  3. Fill in your booking details. Add your pickup date, cargo weight and description, and your contact and drop-off address.
  4. Send the booking request. The route operator reviews your request and confirms the price and pickup.
  5. Hand over your cargo. Drop off or meet the truck on the pickup date and pay only for the space you used.

About CargoFlow

Shipping in the Philippines is expensive for small businesses that can't fill an entire truck. CargoFlow lets you search routes that are already running and book just the space your shipment needs, so you share the trip and the cost instead of paying for the whole vehicle.
