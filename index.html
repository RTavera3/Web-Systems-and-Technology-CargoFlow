Book cargo space going your way

Don't pay for a whole truck. Tell us your route and load — we match a carrier already driving that way and price it by the space you use.

How booking works

  1. Enter your shipment details. Pickup and drop-off locations, pickup date, cargo weight, and what you're sending.
  2. Get matched automatically. CargoFlow assigns a carrier already running that route with room for your load.
  3. See your price. Calculated from the route distance and the space your cargo takes up — no whole-truck fee.
  4. Confirm your booking. Review the match and price, then send the request.
  5. Hand over your cargo. Meet the truck on the pickup date and pay only for your space.

Who it's for

  • SMEs — small and medium businesses with partial-load shipments
  • Online sellers — e-commerce merchants shipping orders to customers
  • Manufacturers — businesses moving goods between locations

About CargoFlow

Shipping in the Philippines is expensive for small businesses that can't fill an entire truck. CargoFlow matches your shipment to a carrier already running that route and prices it by the space your cargo uses, so you share the trip and the cost instead of paying for the whole vehicle.
