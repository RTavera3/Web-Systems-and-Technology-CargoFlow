What happens next

  4. Pay on pick-up — ₱850 in cash or e-wallet. Nothing is charged before then.
  5. Track by SMS until the cargo is delivered.

About CargoFlow

Shipping in the Philippines is expensive for small businesses that can't fill an entire truck. CargoFlow matches your shipment to a carrier already running that route and prices it by the space your cargo uses, so you share the trip and the cost instead of paying for the whole vehicle.
