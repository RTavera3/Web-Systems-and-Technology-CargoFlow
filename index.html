About

CargoFlow is a cargo-sharing platform for the Philippines. It matches shipments that are too small to fill a whole truck with carriers that have unused space on the same route.

Shippers pay for the space they need. Carriers earn from space they would otherwise waste.

How it works

  1. Enter where your cargo is going.
  2. See carriers heading the same way and pick the space you need.
  3. Coordinate with the carrier and share the cost of the trip.

Features

  • Smart matching by route, cargo size, capacity, and schedule
  • Cost sharing between shippers on the same truck
  • Cargo booking
  • Shipment tracking
  • Direct messaging with carriers

Prototype: route data is sample data, and the booking, tracking, and messaging pages are planned, not built.

Who it's for

  • Small and medium businesses
  • Online sellers
  • Manufacturers
  • Independent shippers
  • Local carriers
